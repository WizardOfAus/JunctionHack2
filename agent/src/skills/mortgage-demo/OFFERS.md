# Loan offers and market rates

All offers use the 12-month Euribor plus a margin.

## Reference rates
- 12-month Euribor: 3.328% on 13 November 2026 (euribor-rates.eu; summarum.fi shows the same). Look for a newer value.
- Bank of Finland: average rate on new housing loans 3.54% in September 2026 (release 28 October 2026).
- Bank of Finland: average margin 0.52% (January 2026, the latest published).

## The three demo offers (pass all three to mortgage_calculator)
- Offer A, standard loan with a high margin and fee: margin 0.80%, 25 years, annuity, opening fee 2% of the loan,
  EUR 5 a month. Illustrative. It uses the high end of published 2026 terms (a 0.80% margin in the Taloustaito
  comparison of 19 October 2026, and the top of Hypo's 0.5 to 2.0% fee range).
- Offer B, lowest first payment: margin 0.75%, with 0% for the first 12 months, 40 years, annuity,
  24 months without repayment, no opening fee, EUR 2.50 a month. Illustrative. 40-year loans are legal from
  1 June 2026 (Valtioneuvosto, 7 May 2026), and a 0% margin for 12 months was a real 2026 campaign.
- Offer C, low margin and low fee: margin 0.45%, 25 years, annuity, opening fee 0.4% of the loan, EUR 2.50 a month.
  Nordea's published example terms, 10/2026: EUR 163,000, 24 years, nominal rate 3.758%, effective rate 4.0%,
  fee EUR 652. https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/palvelumme/lainat/asuntolainat/esimerkki.html
