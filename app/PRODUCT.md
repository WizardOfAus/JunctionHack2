# Koti

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: TypeScript, React, Vinext/Vite, server-side financial tools and a private Sites preview. Source remains in WizardOfAus/JunctionHack2.

## Users
People in Finland choosing to understand spending, build a safety net or work towards a first home. This slice demonstrates those goals with one audited synthetic account.

## Product Purpose
Build financial confidence through goal-led exploration while protecting everyday life. Demonstrate a complete, reliable, interactive hackathon journey.

## Brand Commitments
A standalone product compatible with S-Pankki. User requested Brilliant's game-like feel, visual style and interactive teaching, with learn-to-earn inspiration from RabbitHole/Earn. Koti is a provisional working name. No official bank endorsement is claimed.

## Operating Context
Money, Learn, Coach and Plan on phones and desktop, entered through a two-step goal and demo-assumptions flow. Synthetic CUST04859, frozen 1 September 2026 demo clock. Learning is optional and contextual.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One shared deterministic money engine. Source-backed questions, editable scenarios, protective reserve, adaptive challenge, ASP education. Money and XP are separate. Initial rewards are non-monetary; actual sponsored rewards remain open. Real banking and model credentials are not yet configured.

## Evidence on Hand
Pinned Junction CSV downloaded locally; historical targets are in ../docs/build-plan.md and being independently audited. No product customers, testimonials or performance claims exist.

## Product Principles
Explain before teaching. Protect essential spending. Make assumptions inspectable. Reward understanding rather than aggressive saving. Never claim a forecast is a completed transfer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
390 px mobile, keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled controls, sufficient contrast and reduced motion. Allow unknown financial facts and safe recovery.
