# Where Koti's palette came from

Koti's palette was authored for this prototype in response to the requested standalone identity, S-Pankki compatibility and Brilliant-inspired visual learning. It was not imported from an official S-Pankki token package or brand manual. “Compatible” describes visual intent; it is not an endorsement, certified design-system implementation or working bank integration.

Current CSS tokens in `app/app/globals.css`:

| Role | Value | Reason in Koti |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#183f32` | Calm navigation and strong identity |
| Action green | `#387c36` | Primary actions with readable light text |
| Lime | `#d7ef9d` | Selected navigation and learning accents |
| Paper | `#fbfcf8` | Warm, quiet reading surface |
| Lilac | `#e9e0f4` | Learning categories and illustrations |
| Peach | `#f8e3d4` | A second learning/category accent |

The home illustration is original generated artwork with its prompt recorded in [asset provenance](asset-provenance.md). Manrope is self-hosted with its OFL license. Neither is an official S-Pankki asset. Brilliant influenced short interactive teaching, immediate explanatory feedback and visible progress; no Brilliant artwork or component library was copied.

## Comparison checked on 14 November 2026

S-Pankki's design partners describe its digital refresh as an adaptation of S Group's shared design system, with generous whitespace, softer shapes and green used selectively for emphasis. This supports the general visual compatibility of Koti's direction, but does not establish equality of exact colors, fonts or components.

- [Great Apes: S-Pankki digital refresh](https://greatapes.studio/work/s-pankki)
- [HiQ: S-Bank digital service ecosystem](https://hiq.fi/en/case/a-technically-and-visually-compelling-digital-service-ecosystem-for-s-bank/)

Keep Koti's independent identity for now. If official partner branding becomes a requirement, obtain the approved assets/tokens, record the source/version, map them to the existing tokens and recheck contrast. Do not present guessed hex codes as official brand colors.
